# Ledgerline: design notes

## Brief

Who: a payments engineer's public tool. Audience: Stripe engineers and recruiters reading a
GitHub link, plus anyone who has ever had a settlement file disagree with their database.
The site's job: make the engineering legible in ten seconds and let a visitor audit the ledger.

## Concept

**Idea:** Every screen is a security-printed folio. The product proves a ledger balances, so the
site borrows the visual language of the thing that proves money is real: intaglio printing,
guilloche engraving, serial numbers and specimen stamps.

**Metaphor:** A banknote. Thick paper, hairline engraving, one deep ink, one warning colour.

**Signature:** The rosette. A procedural guilloche whose ring count and amplitude come from the
live anomaly score, turning almost imperceptibly at rest and tightening when risk rises.

**Emotion:** quiet authority. It should feel like holding a freshly printed note, not a dashboard.

**Motion verb:** engrave. Lines draw, figures stamp into place, rules settle.

**Screenshot:** the hero: large tabular figures over pale security paper, rosette at right,
serial line under the headline.

## Art direction

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | `#E9EAE2` | page field, cool security paper |
| ink | `#14160F` | type, rules |
| muted | `#5B604F` | secondary copy |
| line | `rgba(20,22,15,.16)` | hairlines, table rules |
| intaglio | `#1D4A3A` | links, active state, positive figures |
| signal | `#A62E18` | drift, anomaly severity, stamps |
| curtain | `#F3F4EC` | raised panels, table headers |

Type: **Gloock** for display (an engraved serif with high contrast), **Archivo** for interface
and body, **JetBrains Mono** for every number, serial and label. Figures are always tabular.

Grid: 12 columns, 24 px gutter, 1560 px max, 32 px page margin (20 px on phones). Sections are
separated by a single hairline and a folio number, not by cards.

Shape: one radius rule. Nothing is rounded except the risk chips and the stamp, both 2 px.

## Layout plan

- Hero: 7/5 asymmetric split, display type left, rosette and live figures right.
- Trial balance: full width table with a narrow marginal note in column 1.
- Incidents: one featured record at 7 columns, the rest as a compact ledger list.
- Stream: full bleed table, hover lifts the row with a moving rule.
- Reconciliation: 5/7 split, narrative left, drift figure and issue rows right.
- Footer: oversized wordmark, colophon in three columns.

## Genericness check

A generic model would have produced a dark "fintech" dashboard with a purple gradient, KPI cards
and a line chart. This is the opposite: paper, ink, no chart, no cards, no glow. The banknote
metaphor is specific to a ledger and drives the rosette, the serial numbers, the specimen stamp
and the typography.

## Constraints written down

- No real merchant data. Every event is synthetic and generated by `buildSeed()` with a fixed
  PRNG seed; the site says so in the footer.
- No invented metrics. The figures on screen are read from the running API.
